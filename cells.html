Compartmentalization Doctrine

Article III — Charter
Contact — Tier 0 Probationary. No cell knowledge.
Cleared — Tier 1 Assigned cell and function known. No cross-cell visibility.
Warranted — Tier 2 Knows multiple cells exist. Limited cross-cell awareness.
Sanctioned — Tier 3 Full cell visibility. Cell lead authority.
Directorate — Tier ∞ Supreme authority. 1–3 members. Single-letter designations.
