Contact Protocol

We Do Not Recruit Broadly

OSR membership is by invitation only. Prospective members are identified, evaluated, and approached at the discretion of existing Warranted-tier operatives and above, subject to Directorate approval. If you believe you have what it takes — demonstrate it. The right people will notice.

01

Operational Competence

Members are expected to maintain a standard of operational competence, personal discipline, and role proficiency consistent with OSR's mandate. We do not carry passengers.

02

Operational Silence

OSR does not claim, confirm, or reference its operations publicly. Members do not discuss sanctioned operations outside their cell. Results are not publicized. Recognition is not sought.

03

No Freelancing

Members do not conduct operations that intersect with OSR's mandate outside of sanctioned activity. Undisclosed intersecting activity is treated as a security breach.

04

Commitment

Advancement is observed and conferred, not requested. No member may self-advocate for tier advancement. Regular participation in org activities is expected.
